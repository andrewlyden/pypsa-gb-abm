Allocation Round 5 (2023) drew no offshore wind bids at an administrative strike price of £44/MWh (2012 prices, £61 in 2024 prices):
bidders needed more than that; it is not in the table because nothing cleared.

Reading: the ratio is what a model starting from published costs would have to learn from each round. It is not stable.
A value estimated on Rounds 1 to 4 would have been wrong by a factor of two for Round 6. The published costs lag
the market in both directions: they were too high while costs fell (2015 to 2022) and too low after costs rose (2023 to 2024).
The 2025 edition is within about 10 % of Round 7 for offshore wind and solar and 20 % below it for onshore wind.
